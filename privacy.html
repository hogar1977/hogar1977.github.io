Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CtrlMail is a desktop mail application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your data, including data it receives from Google APIs.

The short version

  • CtrlMail has no server. Your mail, contacts and settings are stored only on your computer.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • CtrlMail talks only to the services you connect: Google's APIs for Gmail and Google Contacts, and the mail servers of any other accounts you add.

Data CtrlMail accesses

When you connect a Google account and grant access, CtrlMail uses:

  • Gmail (https://mail.google.com/, gmail.labels, gmail.settings.basic): to download your messages so you can read and search them offline, to send mail you write, to apply the changes you make (read, star, label, archive, move, delete, drafts), and to read and change your labels and your out-of-office reply.
  • Google Contacts (contacts, contacts.other.readonly): to show your contacts, to add, edit and delete them when you ask, and to suggest addresses of people you have written to.
  • Your email address: to identify the connected account.

How the data is used and stored

  • All data is used only to provide CtrlMail's features to you, on your computer.
  • Messages, attachments, contacts and the search index are stored in your user folders (for example ~/.local/share/ctrlmail). Settings are stored in ~/.config/ctrlmail.
  • Sign-in tokens and passwords are stored in your system keyring, not in plain files.
  • Backups, if you make them, are written to a folder you choose on your computer.
  • Images and other remote content in messages are blocked until you choose to load them for a message or a sender.

Sharing

CtrlMail does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. The developer never receives it. Data leaves your computer only when you act: sending mail, changing mail or contacts (which updates Google's copy), loading remote images you allowed, or following an unsubscribe link.

CtrlMail has an optional, local interface for programs on your own computer (for example a command-line tool or an AI assistant you run). It is read-only by default, every additional permission is off until you switch it on in Settings, and it is not reachable from other computers. What such a program does with data is up to you and that program.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CtrlMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and is not read by any person other than you.

Removing your data

  • Remove an account in CtrlMail's Settings: its local copy is deleted and CtrlMail's access to that Google account is revoked. You can also delete the folders named above.
  • Revoke CtrlMail's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
