# hogar1977.github.io

The public pages for ClewMail, a personal desktop mail client for Gmail: the homepage, privacy policy and terms of use that Google's sign-in links to.

## Why ClewMail

In the Greek myth, Ariadne gave Theseus a clew, a ball of thread, before he went into the Labyrinth. He unwound it on his way in, and after facing the Minotaur he followed it back out. That thread is where the word "clue" comes from: the old name for a ball of thread came to mean whatever leads you through a puzzle.

A mailbox is a labyrinth of its own, and every conversation in it is a thread. ClewMail lays each one out on a line through time: who wrote, who answered and when, and whose move it is now. Its icon is that thread, a single rope laid out as an envelope. The app was called CtrlMail until November 2026.
