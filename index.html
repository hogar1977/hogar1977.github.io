CtrlMail

A keyboard-first desktop mail client for Linux, with a full offline copy of your mailbox.

CtrlMail is a personal mail app for Gmail and IMAP accounts. It runs entirely on your own computer: a small background service keeps a local copy of your mail and contacts in sync, and a desktop window lets you read, search, write and organise mail with the keyboard.

What it does with your Google account

  • Reads, sends and organises your Gmail (labels, archive, stars, drafts, filters and the out-of-office reply), at your request.
  • Shows and edits your Google Contacts, and suggests addresses you have written to before.
  • Keeps all of this on your computer only. CtrlMail has no server and no account of its own.
