ClewMail

Follow the thread. A keyboard-first desktop mail client for Linux, with a full offline copy of your mailbox.

ClewMail is a personal mail app for Gmail accounts. It runs entirely on your own computer: a small background service keeps a local copy of your mail and contacts in sync, and a desktop window lets you read, search, write and organise mail with the keyboard.

Why ClewMail

In the Greek myth, Ariadne gave Theseus a clew, a ball of thread, before he went into the Labyrinth. He unwound it on his way in, and after facing the Minotaur he followed it back out. That thread is where the word “clue” comes from.

A mailbox is a labyrinth of its own, and every conversation in it is a thread. ClewMail lays each one out on a line through time: who wrote, who answered and when, and whose move it is now. Its icon is that thread, a single rope laid out as an envelope. Clew is said like “clue”; the app was called CtrlMail until November 2026.

What it does with your Google account

  • Reads, sends and organises your Gmail (labels, archive, stars, drafts, filters and the out-of-office reply), at your request.
  • Shows and edits your Google Contacts, and suggests addresses you have written to before.
  • Keeps all of this on your computer only. ClewMail has no server and no account of its own.
